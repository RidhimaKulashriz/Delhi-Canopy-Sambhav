# DelhiCanopy TrustOps — Round 2 Quality Gate

## Purpose

TrustOps is an additive decision-quality layer for DelhiCanopy. It prevents high-impact climate recommendations from being treated as final until the team has completed a repeatable evidence, credibility, challenge, and accountable-review sequence.

## Repeatable Gate

| Stage | Demonstrable action | Required result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Scope | Select a decision such as a ward planting strategy, heat action, or tree-loss response. | A defined decision and location context. |
| 2. Evidence | Record evidence notes or references against mandatory checks. | Every required check is present. |
| 3. Credibility AI | Score evidence for detail, location, dated record, accountable source, and verification method. | Mandatory records reach a credible threshold. |
| 4. Red-team challenge | Record a material challenge such as a utility conflict, land ambiguity, or maintenance gap. | Challenge is either resolved with evidence or kept as a blocker. |
| 5. Accountable review | Assign a human reviewer and record a decision. | Approved for field review, or returned for evidence. |
| 6. Audit receipt | Produce a deterministic readiness score, immutable-style event timeline, and exportable decision receipt. | A clear, reproducible finalization state. |

## Judge Demo Flow

1. Open **TrustOps Quality Gate** from the existing X-Tech navigation and start on the intentionally held decision state.
2. Select a high-impact recommendation and show that incomplete documentation cannot finalize the work.
3. Use **Load Winning Demo** to replay the deterministic evidence packet, credibility scoring, red-team resolutions, and reviewer assignment.
4. Show that the review button only activates after the required quality threshold is met.
5. Approve the field review and copy the decision receipt.
6. Reset the gate to demonstrate that the decision is held again rather than remaining permanently approved.
7. Open the linked Planting Space Finder candidate or AI Planner recommendation to show that TrustOps extends existing decision workflows.

## Safety Boundary

TrustOps is a quality-control and traceability system. It does not validate a legal permit, replace a site visit, guarantee model accuracy, or authorize physical planting.
