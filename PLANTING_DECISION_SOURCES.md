# Planting-space screening and quality gate

The new Site Finder is a **preliminary desktop-screening tool**, not a planting permit. It helps teams prioritize a location, identify missing evidence, and prepare an implementation brief before the responsible land-owning and greening agency approves any work.

Delhi’s Green Action Plan describes plantation as a multi-agency programme and links better outcomes to site-appropriate species, soil assessment, protection, maintenance, watering, and third-party audit. It also lists practical audit indicators including site selection, species choice, planting method, pit and spacing quality, timing, plant health, cultural operations, protection, management, growth and survival, and soil-and-water conservation. The Site Finder turns these into a repeatable, visible quality gate rather than treating a high model score as permission to plant.[1]

The implementation workflow presented in the product follows this sequence: identify a candidate space, confirm land custodian and utilities, perform field and soil checks, match species and planting design, secure approvals and protection, plant in the seasonal window, and monitor survival. The application flags a recommendation as **field verification pending** until all evidence items have been checked by an accountable team.
